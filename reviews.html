Client Reviews: Looking for the Best Realtor in State College, PA?

What clients say about working with Barb Alpert, REALTOR® — Kissinger Bigatel & Brower

The most useful thing you can do before hiring any REALTOR® is talk to people who have actually closed with them. Below are client reviews — and Barb is glad to connect you with past clients directly if you’d like to ask your own questions.

Client reviews

Barb is absolutely phenomenal. My wife and I were buying our first home and had little knowledge of the process or the local market. While looking at the first home of many with Barb, she was very honest with us. She would tell us when a house wasn’t for us, and was very flexible when trying to set up showings. When it was time to make an offer, she continued to fight for our best interest and used her experience to net is a fantastic deal. She continued to answer calls and assist us even after closing. She is truly wonderful and we would recommend her to anyone!

First-time buyers, State College · Client review via 1kbb.com

Barb was my agent when I began my journey to become a first-time homeowner. From our first meeting I was impressed with Barb’s professionalism and knowledge. She guided this novice through this long and arduous, and often frustrating process of buying a house. Barb was incredibly patient with me as I tried to determine what I wanted and what I didn’t. She was understanding when I got cold feet on a property I came very close to buying. She was always prompt when responding to my requests and arranged showings that accommodated my schedule. When I finally found “the house” Barb was a savvy and persistent negotiator who I trusted implicitly to protect my best interests. She truly went above and beyond to make the purchase happen and I have been happily living in my new home for a little over a month now. Barb has continued to check-in to make sure things are going well. I would highly recommend Barb to anyone. You will not be disappointed.

First-time buyer · Client review via 1kbb.com

If you need a realtor, look no further! Barb is the best of the best. She is concise, thorough, efficient, and truly takes an interest in helping you find your dream home. She makes her self readily available 24/7 and doesn’t take no for an answer. She makes sure you have everything you want and/or need and quickly! She is very knowledgeable about all kinds of houses, properties, locations, and contacts. If you have a question, she will get you an answer. She is very friendly, polite and personable. She loves to help and will go above and beyond anything you would ever expect. She makes you feel special and treats you like you are the only client she has even though she has many. She is very helpful with showings, and gives an honest opinion but not so as to sway your decision. She finds houses that are available that you have no idea about and has all of the latest information about what’s on the market. She is truly one of the nicest people we have ever met and would recommend her to ANYBODY. She is wonderful to work with, especially if you’re a first time home buyer. We had NO idea what we needed to do and she answered all of our questions and gave us information and advice we didn’t even know we needed. Even after we closed, she continued to help us with some questions we had. We are currently writing this review from the comfy couch of our new beautiful home and we couldn’t be happier.
