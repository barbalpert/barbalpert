The Alpert & Rupert Real Estate Team: A Mother-Son Team in State College, PA

Barb Alpert, REALTOR®, Associate Broker & Cliff Rupert, REALTOR® — both with Kissinger Bigatel & Brower

Why did Barb build a mother-son team?

The honest answer: pace. Good properties in Happy Valley can come and go in a weekend, and sellers need a listing prepared, priced and marketed without a two-week lag. Rather than hand clients off to an assistant, Barb teamed up with someone she trusts completely — her son, Cliff Rupert.

The result is two licensed REALTORS® on your file. Barb brings more than 30 years of transaction experience and Associate Broker-level judgment. Cliff brings energy, availability and a focus on client care. Between them, someone can almost always get to the phone, the property, or the closing table.

Cliff Rupert, REALTOR® — State College native

Cliff graduated from State College Area High School and earned a B.S. in Recreation, Parks and Tourism Management from Penn State. After graduation he worked as an outdoor educator across the American Southwest, guiding groups through some genuinely demanding country — a job that rewards preparation, patience and reading people well.

Back home, he’s connected to the community through cycling and environmental protection. He holds several water and wastewater management licenses and helps protect a well-regarded local trout fishery. If you’re buying a property on a well and septic system, or one with a stream running through it, that background is more useful than it may first appear.
