Bellefonte PA Real Estate: Victorian Homes in the County Seat

From Barb Alpert, REALTOR®, Associate Broker — Kissinger Bigatel & Brower

Bellefonte is the county seat and, architecturally, the most interesting town in Centre County. Built up during the iron era and never torn down, it’s a hillside of Victorian houses, a limestone courthouse, and a big spring that gives the town its name — about fifteen minutes northeast of State College.

What is Bellefonte known for?

Its architecture, first. The historic district is dense with Victorian and Federal-era buildings, many of them substantial homes with the woodwork, stained glass and staircases that come with that period. The town also has genuine historical weight: several Pennsylvania governors came from Bellefonte, and the courthouse has anchored the square since the county’s early days.

Then there’s the water. Spring Creek runs right through Talleyrand Park in the middle of town, and it’s one of the better-known wild trout streams in the eastern United States — a genuine draw for anglers. The Big Spring feeds it at the edge of the park.

What kind of homes are in Bellefonte?

  • Historic Victorians in and around the borough — large, detailed, and often offering more square footage per dollar than anything comparable closer to campus. Budget for the maintenance an old house genuinely requires.
  • Modest borough homes on the surrounding streets: solid, walkable, and among the more attainable entry points into Centre County.
  • Newer development in Spring and Benner Townships around the borough, with easy access to I-99 and Route 26.
  • Country properties and small farms in the surrounding townships — good limestone ground, and often better value per acre than the Boalsburg side. See farms & equestrian.

What schools serve Bellefonte?

The borough and much of the surrounding area are served by the Bellefonte Area School District, a separate district from State College Area. Confirm the attendance boundary for any specific address with the district directly before relying on it.

Why buy or sell in Bellefonte?

Buyers: this is where Centre County value lives. If you want character and space and you’re willing to drive fifteen or twenty minutes to campus, your budget goes considerably further here. Have an inspector who knows old houses. See the buying process.

Sellers: historic homes need marketing that speaks to the buyer who wants one — photography that shows the details, and copy that gets the period and the updates right. That buyer often comes from outside the county. See the selling process.

Also comparing State College or Boalsburg? We’ll walk through the tradeoffs honestly.
